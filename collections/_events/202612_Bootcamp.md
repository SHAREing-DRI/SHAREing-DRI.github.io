Do you want to maximise the impact from a new or existing initiative? Are you wondering how you can effectively evidence this impact, and demonstrate this to yourself and others?

The Maximising and Evidencing Impact Bootcamp is for digital technical professionals looking to develop their understanding, skills and expertise in purposeful project design and evaluation, whether that is a one-off event or a large-scale research programme.
The training will involve interactive activities, facilitated discussion and access to research, frameworks and resources to support your project development. 
Ideally, bootcamp participants should have their own project they would like to design and evaluate to ensure learning can be applied directly in a context relevant and applicable to you. However, illustrative examples from different areas will also be introduced throughout the course. 
In addition to the training, participants will also have access to ongoing drop-in support and be part of a community of practice to share learning with other participants. 

The bootcamp will be delivered by expert facilitators (Helen Cramman and Lorraine Coghill) who have extensive experience of delivering CPD to research professionals as well as designing and evaluating initiatives. The bootcamp will be delivered in partnership with the Durham Centre for Academic Development, Science Engagement Team and Durham University Centre for CPD and Outreach and will include contributions from a range of technical professional colleagues and evaluators as part of the bootcamp.

If you have any questions about the Bootcamp, please contact Helen Cramman at researcher.development@durham.ac.uk.

# Programme content

Core topics will include:
-	Introduction to evaluation and purposeful design for impact
-	Theory of Change development
-	Measuring impact and implementation strategies
-	Evaluation design and core research methods
-	Ethics and working with people
-	Data governance and management (including personal data)
-	Project management fundamentals
-	Data analysis principles
-	Dissemination and communication of findings


# Activities and Delivery

Each day will be structured around a blend of:

-	Short teaching sessions introducing key concepts
-	Facilitated workshops and practical exercises
-	Individual and small-group project development time
-	Peer feedback and discussion sessions


Following the bootcamp, participants will have access to:

-	Optional drop-in support sessions for continued guidance.
-	A community of practice, enabling ongoing peer learning, collaboration, and knowledge exchange.
