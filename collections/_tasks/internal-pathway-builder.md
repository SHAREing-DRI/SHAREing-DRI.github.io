## Fit to programme

This work contributes to WP3 (Professional Skills), exploring how professional development opportunities can be made more accessible, relevant and responsive to individual learning needs.

Instead of focusing solely on the delivery of individual training courses, the initiative explores a more holistic approach to professional development: supporting individuals in identifying their learning needs, navigating the existing training landscape and bringing together relevant opportunities into personalised learning pathways.

A key principle underpinning this approach is to build on and signpost existing training provision, rather than duplicating materials or recreating resources that are already available elsewhere. By connecting existing resources and encouraging individuals to take ownership of their learning journeys, the approach aims to support more flexible, sustainable and personalised professional development across the Digital Research Infrastructure community.

## Description

A first prototype of a Learning Pathway Builder has been developed to explore how individuals can discover, curate and organise training opportunities according to their professional development needs.

The prototype brings together existing training resources into a searchable catalogue and provides functionality to organise learning opportunities into personalised training lists and pathways. It also explores how curated learning pathways can be used to guide individuals through relevant topics and resources, while allowing them to adapt these suggestions to their own interests, experience and learning objectives.

The prototype includes the following features:

- **Training catalogue:** A central place to discover existing training opportunities and learning resources.
- **Calendar:** An overview of upcoming training events and opportunities.
- **Personal training list:** The ability to collect and organise courses and resources of interest.
- **Personalised learning pathways:** The ability to build individual learning journeys around specific development needs.
- **Curated learning pathways:** Suggested collections of relevant resources to provide starting points for professional development.
- **Personalisation:** Flexibility to adapt pathways and training lists to individual interests and requirements.
- **Adding external courses:** The ability to incorporate additional training opportunities and resources beyond those already included in the catalogue.

This first prototype establishes a foundation for exploring a more learner-centred approach to professional skills development within SHAREing, with scope for further development and refinement based on feedback and user needs.
